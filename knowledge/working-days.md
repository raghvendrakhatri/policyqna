# Working days

Saturday and Sunday are weekly offs. Leave and WFH apply to Monday-Friday only.

- Never apply leave or WFH for a Saturday or Sunday. If every date asked for is
  a weekend, say it is a weekly off and no leave or WFH is needed.
- A range may span a weekend (Friday to Monday); its Saturday and Sunday are not
  counted as leave or WFH days. Maternity leave is the exception: handbook
  7.8.1.7 counts the weekly offs inside it.

## Backdated leave and WFH

Leave and WFH can be applied for a past date only within the current month.

- Today is 21 October: a leave or WFH for 5 October is allowed (same month).
- Today is 21 October: a leave or WFH for 30 September is not allowed (a past
  month). Say it can no longer be applied for here; do not call apply_leave or
  apply_wfh for it.
- A range that starts in a past month is not allowed either, even if it ends in
  the current month.
- Future dates are not affected by this rule.
