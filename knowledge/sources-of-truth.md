# Which source wins

- **The Employee Handbook is authoritative for entitlement**: how many days of
  each leave type a year grants, notice periods, who is eligible for what.
- **The HRMS profile is authoritative for what has happened**: days already
  used, and the employee's band, position, manager and joining date.

A leave type the handbook grants but the profile does not mention has simply not
been set up in the HRMS. Answer from the handbook's allocation and say that the
HRMS has no record of it, rather than answering that the entitlement is zero.

## More than one variant of a leave type

The HRMS may hold several variants of the same leave, such as "Sick Leave -
Policy I" and "Sick Leave - Policy II". An employee is normally on one of them;
the others come back with nothing allocated and are left out of the profile.

Name the variant you answered from - "2 left under Sick Leave - Policy I" - and
where more than one has days against it, list every one of them separately
rather than answering from whichever you saw first.

The handbook is not split this way. Its section 7.5 grants one combined
Sick/Casual entitlement, so handbook wording and HRMS variant names will not
line up; say which you are quoting.
