# Glossary

Abbreviations used in questions and in the policy documents. Expand these
silently — answer with the full term, not the abbreviation.

- PA — Performance Allowance
- WFH — Work From Home
- MMT — MakeMyTrip
- Sr1 — Senior 1 (the Silver band)
- Sr2 — Senior 2 (the Gold band)

Seniority bands, lowest to highest:

- Bronze — Junior / Mid
- Silver — Senior 1 (Sr1)
- Gold — Senior 2 (Sr2)
- Platinum — Leads and above

The perks and benefits each band receives are in the Perks & Benefits 2026–2027
document, retrieved as context when a question needs it.

## City tiers

Travel, accommodation and daily allowances depend on a city's tier. Annexure B
of the Employee Handbook classifies every city, but its column headings sit on
one page and the city lists on the next, so the headings rarely arrive with the
names. The mapping is:

- **Tier 1** is exactly these eight: Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata, Bangalore,
  Hyderabad, Ahmedabad, Pune.
- **Tier 2** is the long list that follows them in Annexure B, running from Agra
  to Warangal - Indore, Jaipur, Udaipur, Lucknow, Surat and so on.
- **Tier 3** is any Indian city in neither list.
- A city outside India is not tiered; international travel is "to be decided".

Say which tier you placed a city in. If it is not in the Tier 1 list and you
cannot see it in Annexure B, say so rather than assuming Tier 3.


## Position decides the band

The band follows from the position, so work it out whenever the position,
designation or title is known - it is not a guess:

| Position | Band |
|---|---|
| Junior, or Mid-level | Bronze |
| Senior 1 | Silver |
| Senior 2 | Gold |
| Lead, and anything above a Lead | Platinum |

Match the position however it is written. "Senior Engineer 2", "Sr. Engineer II",
"SE2" and "Sr2" all mean Senior 2, so Gold. Tech Lead, Team Lead, Engineering
Manager, Head and Director are all Platinum. A title with no level in it, such
as "Engineer", does not identify a band on its own.

State the band you derived and the position you derived it from.

## Do not infer the band from a number

Several bands share the same entitlement, so a figure does not identify a band.
A 30-day WFH allocation is Silver *or* Gold; 40,000 relocation is every band.

If the band is not stated outright, say which bands the figure is consistent
with, and give the answer for each. Never pick one and answer as if it were
settled - the food allowance differs between Silver and Gold.
