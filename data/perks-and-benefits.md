## Daily Travel & Food Allowance, per day

This depends on both the band and the tier of the city. Read the row for the
band, then the column for the city tier.

| Band | Tier 3 city | Tier 2 city | Tier 1 city |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bronze | 750 | 1,200 | 1,600 |
| Silver | 750 | 1,200 | 1,600 |
| Gold | 1,000 | 1,500 | 1,900 |
| Platinum | 1,000 | 1,600 | 2,000 |

## Accommodation, per day

Also by band and city tier. The sheet gives one hotel rating per band, applying
to every city tier.

| Band | Tier 3 city | Tier 2 city | Tier 1 city |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bronze | 2–3 Star | 2–3 Star | 2–3 Star |
| Silver | 3 Star | 3 Star | 3 Star |
| Gold | 3 Star | 3 Star | 3 Star |
| Platinum | 3–4 Star | 3–4 Star | 3–4 Star |

## Notes
- Bronze and Silver receive no team or vertical meal allowance.
- Relocation Allowance is 40,000 at every band.
- The WFH numbers match the Employee Handbook section 9.3.3, which gives them
  as days per year. The handbook is the authority on how they may be used —
  notice periods, carry-forward, half days.
- The Performance Allowance percentages are rates only. The source sheet does
  not say what amount they are a percentage of.
